# F — assets/Arenas/CREDITS.txt: origin of the tile-built battle arenas (merge the summary into the root CREDITS.txt).

TILE-BUILT BATTLE ARENAS (assets/Arenas)
========================================
What:     28 top-down battle floors (12 field arenas: route, grove, cave, icecave, beach, sea, desert, mountain, town,
          indoor, lab, league; 16 gym floors gym-<town>), 1600 x 1152 px each, generated by tools/arenas/build-arenas.mjs.
          sea (먼 바다, battles that start while surfing): the open water, the sand island (its corner, edge and inside
          cells repeated to 10 x 6 and 4 x 4 tiles) and the 2 x 2 sea rocks of 19번수로 (map 40, the pack's "NOANIMSEA"
          autotile and its tileset), placed by the recipe; the moving wave glints are drawn by the game at run time.
Source:   every 32 x 32 tile is copied whole from the converted overworld maps (public/data/maps) and their tile art
          (assets/Overworld/tilesets and autotiles), i.e. the user's Another Red resources (a local copy of the
          original source: resources/Graphics, Tilesets and Autotiles). Which map cells make which arena:
          tools/arenas/recipes.mjs. No tile was drawn or edited by hand; ring and scenery tiles of halls are darkened.
Authors:  community fan-game tileset and autotile artists; the resources carry no per-file credits, so no individual
          author can be named here. Pokemon places and designs belong to Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK inc.
Use:      unofficial, non-commercial fan project only (see the root CREDITS.txt).
Rebuild:  node tools/arenas/build-arenas.mjs   (check: --check). Run it after tools/build-data.mjs, which rewrites
          public/data/arenas.json without these entries.
