# 플레이어 트레이너 원화

[남녀 트레이너 원화 시트](player-trainers.png)는 이 게임용으로 ImageGen에서 생성한 그림이다. 공식 《포켓몬스터 레츠고! 피카츄·레츠고! 이브이》의 주인공과 《브릴리언트 다이아몬드·샤이닝 펄》의 광휘·빛나를 디자인 참고 자료로 삼았다. [레츠고 공식 트레이너 일러스트](https://pokemonletsgo.pokemon.com/en-us/story/index.html) · [신오 공식 주인공 소개](https://diamondpearl.pokemon.com/en-gb/story/)

빨간 챙모자와 노란 배낭, 노란 반다나와 청록색 숄더백으로 두 사람의 실루엣을 구분한다. 두 캐릭터 모두 몬스터볼을 쥔 앞모습과 오른쪽을 향한 대기·지시 자세가 있다. 게임용 리소스는 [도트 변환 도구](../build-player-art.py)로 원화에서 만든다.

## 생성 프롬프트

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a usable transparent PIXEL-ART SPRITE SHEET for a Pokémon fan game. Create ONE landscape 3:2 sheet with exactly SIX completely separate full body sprites in an invisible, regular 3-column x 2-row grid. Two original young Pokémon protagonist designs. Classic Game Freak Nintendo DS Pokémon HeartGold/SoulSilver, Platinum and Black/White TRAINER BATTLE SPRITE art direction. Use the expressive heroic ball-hand gesture, colorful sporty travel gear, animate face, deliberate large light-dark pixel clusters and high-readability brimmed cap silhouette seen in official Let's Go and Sinnoh player characters. Draw new people and new wardrobe combinations. This should look like attractive, polished actual handheld Pokémon spritework, not a generic fantasy RPG villager or stiff adult fashion plate. Compact, approximately 3 heads tall including the headwear, big expressive anime eyes, clear noses. Draw as if on native 64x64 grid per character, crisp thick single-dark-pixel outer keyline, no anti-aliasing or painterly texture, classic handheld color ramps and strong chroma, boots and hands clearly readable when tiny.
Top entire row = same boy in three views: energetic dark indigo spiky hair, signature vermilion flat-brim cap with an ivory front flash and small dark diamond patch, cream and petrol-teal sporty windbreaker, dark shorts, red trainers, warm yellow compact backpack and visible Poké Ball. Bottom entire row = same girl in three views: rich chestnut hair flaring into swept twin low tufts, large golden-yellow tied bandana headwrap with two pointed ends pointing up-left, deep marine sleeveless vest over ivory short sleeves, coral red athletic shorts, blue ankle socks and hi-tops, small turquoise field bag and visible Poké Ball. Distinct silhouettes. Confident youthful character acting.
Columns: 1. Stylish full-body near-front three-quarter introduction, facing the viewer, charismatic pose showing a red-and-white Poké Ball in hand. 2. Same person in relaxed battle-ready side three-quarter stance facing strictly screen RIGHT, both shoes planted, ball held close to belt. 3. Same person, side three-quarter facing screen RIGHT, same two planted feet, lunging shoulder forward extending their ball hand emphatically towards the right to command a Pokémon, arm at around shoulder height. Use matching body heights, palette, gear, handedness, hairstyle and clothes across each row. In each cell keep entire silhouette incl cap, fingertips and both soles visible, occupying about 76% cell height, with empty gutters between cells. True transparent background, clear alpha all around; isolated sprites. No shadows on the ground, no text, no lines, no panels, no symbols around the characters, no grid, no labels and no Pokémon on the sheet.
```
